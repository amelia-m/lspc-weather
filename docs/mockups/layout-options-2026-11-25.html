LSPC Weather: three layout options

Mock-ups for the maintainer, 2026-11-25. Sample figures, not live weather. Drawn at phone size first, since that is where the page is read at the drop zone. In every option the flags ("Conditions to note") stay at the top of each weather page: a layout that needs a tap to reach them would let silence read as an all-clear.

A. A landing page of tiles

The plain address opens a menu. Each tile opens a short page of two or three cards, with a way back.

For

  • The whole site is visible on one screen; nothing is found by scrolling.
  • Each page is short, so a card is never far down.
  • Room to grow: a new tool is one more tile.

Against

  • The wind against the limit, the day's first question, is now one tap away. It was the first thing on the page.
  • Every move between sections goes through Home: two taps, where a bar needs one.
  • A figure on a tile is a second place for every caveat the card carries (age, station distance).

B. One page, with icons to jump between sections

The page stays as it is: one scroll per tab. A bar of five icons, each with its word, stays on screen and jumps to a section; the one in view is highlighted. On a phone it sits at the bottom, in reach of a thumb; on a wide screen it becomes a rail down the side.

For

  • Nothing moves: the wind and the flags are still the first thing on the page, and every link and habit keeps working.
  • One tap to any section, from anywhere on the page.
  • The smallest change of the three to build and to test.

Against

  • The page is as long as it is now; the bar shortens the trip, not the page.
  • A bar at the bottom takes about 50 px of a phone's height for good.
  • Three tabs across the top and five icons across the bottom are two rows of navigation to learn.

C. Both: a bottom bar of short pages, opening on "Now"

The bar is the navigation: five places, each a short page. The plain address opens "Now", which is the flags and the surface wind, so there is no menu between the reader and the first question. "More" is the tile menu from option A, for everything that is not today's weather.

For

  • The first screen is still the wind and the flags, and it fits without a long scroll.
  • One tap between any two sections, and each page is short.
  • One row of navigation, where option B has two.

Against

  • The largest change: every card is re-homed, and the Jumpers and Pilots tabs go away as tabs (Pilots moves under "More", or takes a slot).
  • Cards that read together today on one scroll (surface wind beside the winds aloft) are a tap apart.
  • Old links to "#pilots" and the rest need to keep landing somewhere sensible.
