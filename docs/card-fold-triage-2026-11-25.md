## The rule applied

- **Stays in view:** a warning or a caveat that changes how a figure is read or acted on; a limit and its source; what a report cannot show; a standing note that replaced a removed flag; a dated fact about the drop zone that a figure rests on; a data credit and its link; the line that says what a control does.
- **Folds (her rule):** how one data source compared with another, and how a figure is worked out.
- **Her call (neither class):** a key to reading a table. The Hourly wind card's key is already folded, so there is a precedent.
