How these are counted, and adding data

Each hour pairs KPMV's report nearest the top of the hour (within 10 minutes) with the newest NWS ceiling issued at least 0, 6 or 24 hours before it, from NOAA's NDFD archive, which matched api.weather.gov's ceilingHeight value for value when checked. A ceiling is the lowest broken, overcast or vertical-visibility base. Both sides are held to 12,000 ft, the most an AWOS reports. Percentiles are nearest rank; the median averages the middle two.

Grouped by the forecast's band, the rows are what a reader of the forecast faced. Grouped by the reported band, the rows lean by construction: a 300 ft report can be missed upward by thousands of feet but downward by 300 at most.

To add data, fetch a later window and regenerate the file this page reads, then republish it beside the page: npx tsx scripts/ceilingChartData.ts --ceiling <ceiling archives…> --cover <cloud-cover archives…> --out data/parity/ceiling-errors.json. Each flag takes any number of files, and a record in two of them counts once. The method is in docs/ceiling-heights.md in the lspc-weather repository.
