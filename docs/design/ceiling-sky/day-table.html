About these forecasts

NWS: the gridpoint forecast for the DZ, hourly, the same one usairnet shows; its ceiling runs about a day and a half. NBM: the National Blend of Models’ bulletins for KPMV through the relay, the hourly one (base to 25 h) and the three-hourly one (6 to 72 h, the one usairnet’s three-day table reads). The base is the lowest layer whether or not it is a ceiling. Two forecasts, never blended; neither raises a flag. How each compared with KPMV, April to October 2026 …
