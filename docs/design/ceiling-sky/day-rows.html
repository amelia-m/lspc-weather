About these forecasts

NWS: the gridpoint forecast for the DZ, hourly; its ceiling runs about a day and a half. NBM: the National Blend of Models’ bulletins for KPMV through the relay, the three-hourly one 6 to 72 h, the one usairnet’s three-day table reads; its base is the lowest layer whether or not it is a ceiling. Two forecasts, never blended; neither raises a flag. …
